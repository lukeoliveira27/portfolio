FlowBoard

A work management platform I conceived, designed and coded. It started as a way to organise Talenses Group's Communications team and grew into a product: board, daily, editorial calendar, plans, a request portal and focus features each person can switch on if they want. I also made the brand, design system, website and emails.

My role
Concept, product, UX/UI, brand and development
Deliverables
Web and mobile app, website, request portal, design system, system emails
Tools

Visit FlowBoard ↗

The challenge

The Communications team serves seven group brands at once. Requests came in by email, Teams and hallway chats, and nobody had a single view of what was late, waiting for approval or stuck.

Off-the-shelf tools were either too generic or became one more system to feed. I wanted something that organised the work without weighing it down, including for people who get distracted easily or struggle to get started.

The process

01

I started by making the request the centre of everything: board, table, calendar, daily and projects are just different ways of looking at the same thing. Every new screen has to connect to requests instead of becoming a separate system.

02

I created the “graphite and sky” identity: graphite, sky blue and beige, with an orange used only in small brand details. Status colours (green, amber, blue, grey and red) are fixed and never mixed with the brand.

Everything became a Figma design system, with light and dark mode variables mirroring the code.

03

Focus features are optional and described by situation, without labels: gentle nudges, suggestions, calm mode and comfortable reading. Each person switches on only what helps.

The outcome

Today FlowBoard runs the team's daily work, from incoming request to delivery, and has its own website, plans and transactional emails.

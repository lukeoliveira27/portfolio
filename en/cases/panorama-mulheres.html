Instituto Talenses Group × Insper · 2025–2026

Panorama Mulheres

Website for the study that has tracked women's leadership in Brazil since 2017. I organised data, insights and past editions into a clear experience, and created the design system and the “Participe!” campaign for the 6th edition.

My role
Web design, design system and campaign
Deliverables
Website, insights page, editions timeline, design system, banner and posts
Tools
Figma · Photoshop · Illustrator
See it live ↗

The challenge

Panorama Mulheres is a study by Instituto Talenses Group and Insper's Gender Studies Centre. The data is rich, but it was scattered across PDFs and e-books from several editions.

The website had to tell the study's story, surface the right numbers and invite companies to take part and support it.

The process

01

I layered the page: purpose, the study in numbers, the edition's key insights and clear calls to download, respond and support.

02

The timeline brings together every edition since 2017 and the C-Talks episode about the study, presenting the historical series as an asset of the Institute.

03

I turned it all into a Figma design system with components and variables, and used it for the “Participe!” campaign of the 6th edition.

The outcome

The website became the study's home: it holds the historical series, the 5th edition insights and the entry point for companies to join the next one.

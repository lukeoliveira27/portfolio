Summer Job Moove

Bilingual (PT/EN) landing page for Moove's summer programme for Brazilians studying abroad. I did the design, code, SEO and launch kit: marketing emails, static pieces and motion for social.

My role
Design, development, SEO and launch kit
Deliverables
PT/EN landing page, 2 emails, feed, stories and landscape pieces, motion in 3 formats
Tools
Figma · HTML/CSS/JS · Replit · After Effects
See it live ↗

The challenge

Moove, one of South America's largest lubricant companies, wanted to attract Brazilian students at universities abroad for a 6 to 8 week summer immersion. Mappit runs the selection.

The audience reads in two languages, decides on their phone and needed to understand the programme and reach the application in a few taps.

The process

01

I followed Moove's brandbook (DM Sans, official lime green and blue) and structured the page as a conversation: who Moove is, what the programme is, profile, what you get, steps and FAQ.

02

I coded the site in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with the English version generated automatically from the Portuguese page, light and dark mode, automated tests, security headers and UTM links on every button to track where each application comes from.

03

The launch kit speaks the same language: two emails (one for the general base and one for students abroad), feed, stories and landscape pieces, and motion in all three formats.

The outcome

It took seven days from Moove's brief to the live site, client review included, published on the official domain.
